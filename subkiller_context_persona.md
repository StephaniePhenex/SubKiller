# SubKiller (The Honest Sub) - Context & Persona

## 1. Problem Statement
Modern consumers silently hemorrhage money through overlapping and underutilized recurring subscriptions, blinded by the "Subjective Perception Gap"—the cognitive disconnect between a service's perceived utility and its actual financial drain. Current personal finance tools fail them by demanding tedious manual data entry, complex spreadsheet management, or forced budgeting rules. Consequently, users avoid auditing their subscriptions entirely due to the high cognitive friction, remaining trapped in a cycle of passive spending and financial anxiety without a clear, immediate understanding of their wasted capital.

## 2. Target User Persona
The Financially Fatigued Digital Native (Ages 18–35). Highly subscribed to various streaming platforms, SaaS tools, and lifestyle apps, yet overwhelmed by financial admin. They possess a strong desire to cut wasteful spending but have zero tolerance for manual data entry or complex budgeting interfaces. They respond best to low-friction interactions, blunt reality checks, and the immediate psychological gratification of gamified savings.

## 3. Core Value Proposition
A frictionless, AI-driven financial reality check that leverages voice input and "What-If" simulations to instantly expose the true cost of subscription waste. By discarding rigid budget tracking in favor of a brutalist "Subscription Graveyard" and AI-generated Importance Scores, it transforms the dreaded chore of financial auditing into a highly satisfying, low-effort act of reclaiming personal wealth.
